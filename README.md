# Physics TV

Science professors explain physics, do experiments, and entertain people using science.

## Daily channel format

- **12:00 AM–6:00 AM:** Midnight Synth — long-form synth music for the overnight lab.
- **6:00 AM–6:00 PM:** Physics Lab — professors, experiments, classical mechanics, quantum mechanics, relativity, forces, motion, and The Mechanical Universe.
- **6:00 PM–12:00 AM:** Physics at the Movies — physics- and science-related feature films in full evening blocks.

Physics TV uses the same synchronized channel scaffolding as the other Infinity TV stations: Join Live, Start Over, Rewind 30 seconds, a seven-day guide, channel remote, share rewards, and a lineup that changes at **12:00 AM in the viewer's local time** without reshuffling when an individual video source fails.
